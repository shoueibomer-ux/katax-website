ABOUT KATAX

Accounting built on accuracy, not shortcuts.

Kaya for Accounting & Tax Services Inc. (KATAX) is a Toronto-based firm handling personal, self-employment, business, and corporate tax preparation, GST/HST filing, and bookkeeping.

Our mission

To provide accurate, reliable, and personalized accounting and tax services that simplify our clients' financial responsibilities and support their success.

We deliver professional personal, self-employment, business, and corporate tax preparation, GST/HST filing, bookkeeping, and accounting services with integrity, confidentiality, and attention to detail.

We build long-term relationships with our clients by understanding their unique financial needs, maintaining compliance, identifying opportunities, and providing practical financial guidance they can trust.

Our vision

To become a trusted and respected accounting and tax services firm known for accuracy, integrity, professionalism, and exceptional client service.

We aim to empower individuals, entrepreneurs, self-employed professionals, and businesses with clear financial information and dependable tax and accounting solutions — enabling confident decisions, sustainable growth, and a stronger financial future.

At KATAX, we envision a future where accounting and taxes are not a source of stress, but a foundation for our clients' financial confidence and success.
