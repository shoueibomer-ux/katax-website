SERVICES

Every filing, one accountable file.

From a single personal return to ongoing corporate bookkeeping, each service below is handled by the same person, start to finish.

T1

Personal income tax returns

E-filed personal returns for individuals and families — reviewed for accuracy before submission, not just run through software and sent.

  • Employment, pension, and investment income
  • Credits and deductions you're entitled to (tuition, medical, RRSP, and more)
  • Prior-year adjustments and CRA correspondence support
T2125

Self-employment tax returns

For freelancers, contractors, gig workers, and sole proprietors reporting business or professional income alongside a personal return.

  • Business-use-of-home and vehicle expense calculations
  • Income and expense reconciliation from your records or receipts
  • Guidance on what to track for next year
T2

Business income tax returns

Corporate tax return preparation and filing, prepared alongside your bookkeeping so the numbers match without a last-minute scramble.

  • Corporate T2 preparation and e-filing
  • Year-end coordination with your bookkeeping
  • Filing deadline and instalment tracking
GL

Complete accounting & bookkeeping service

Ongoing bookkeeping so your books are current and reconciled all year — not reconstructed under deadline pressure.

  • Monthly or quarterly transaction entry and reconciliation
  • Financial statements you can actually read
  • Records kept audit-ready throughout the year
GST34

GST/HST returns

Registration, filing, and reconciliation for GST/HST-registered businesses and self-employed individuals.

  • GST/HST registration guidance for new businesses
  • Return preparation on your filing schedule (monthly, quarterly, or annual)
  • Input tax credit reconciliation
